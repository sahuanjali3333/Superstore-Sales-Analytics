# Superstore Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard
An end-to-end Data Analysis project examining retail store performance, customer behavior, and regional sales trends to drive data-informed business decisions.

##  Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Preprocessing & EDA:** Python (Pandas, NumPy), Google Colab
* **Data Visualization & Dashboard:** Power BI, Microsoft Excel

##  Project Overview & Key Insights
* **Total Revenue:** Analyzed over 9,800+ retail transactions generating a total turnover of **₹2.26M**.
* **Seasonal Trends:** Identified clear sales peaks during Q4 (September to December), indicating high holiday/festive purchasing behavior.
* **Top Categories:** **Technology** and **Furniture** drove maximum revenue, while **Office Supplies** captured the highest order volume (approx. 60%).
* **Regional Performance:** The **West** and **East** regions emerged as the strongest markets, highlighting potential areas for growth in Central and South regions.
